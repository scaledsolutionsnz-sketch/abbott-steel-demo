Key Specialisation

Earthquake Strengthening

Keeping Buildings
Safe & Standing

Earthquake strengthening is a major part of what Abbott Steel does. We design and install structural steel retrofits that bring older buildings up to current seismic standards — protecting people, property, and investment.

Seismic Retrofitting

Steel frames and bracing installed into existing structures to meet NZ seismic standards.

Steel Moment Frames

Rigid steel moment frames engineered to absorb and distribute seismic loads effectively.

Bracing Systems

Diagonal steel bracing to resist lateral forces and prevent building racking during seismic events.

IEP Compliance Work

Structural steel to meet engineer-specified requirements and bring buildings to target %NBS ratings.

About Abbott Steel

Tom Abbott & His Team.
Quality You Can Count On.

Abbott Steel is a New Zealand-owned fabrication business run by Tom Abbott and his team of certified welders. We take pride in doing things properly — quality steel, solid welds, and workmanship that holds up. Whether your project is straightforward or complex, it gets the same care and attention every time.
