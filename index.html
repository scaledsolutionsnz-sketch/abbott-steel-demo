What We Do

Steel Solutions for
Every Project

From rural sheds to structural frames, Abbott Steel handles the full scope of steel fabrication — custom, precise, and built to handle New Zealand conditions.

Structural Steel

Beams, columns, frames, and bracing for commercial and rural buildings. Engineered to spec and built to carry the load.

Farm Sheds & Buildings

Hay barns, implement sheds, and agricultural buildings built tough for the demands of New Zealand farming.

Custom Fabrication

One-off and short-run steel fabrication to your drawings or ours. Brackets, frames, platforms, and everything in between.

Gates & Fencing

Farm gates, driveway entrances, and steel post fencing. Heavy-duty builds designed to last decades in rural New Zealand.

Trailer Fabrication

Custom steel trailers built to carry your load. Farm, equipment, and general-purpose trailers built right here in New Zealand.
