Before You Call

Common Questions

Where are you based?

Our workshop is at 722 Kaikorai Valley Road, Burnside, Dunedin. Give Tom a call to talk about where your job is and whether it suits a workshop build, an on-site install, or both.

How do I get a quote?

Call Tom on 027 458 7733 (Monday to Friday, 7am to 4:30pm) or email us. Plans, engineer's drawings, photos and rough measurements all help us give you an accurate price.

Can you work from my engineer's or architect's plans?

Yes. We're happy to build from your plans or work the design out from scratch with you. For structural and seismic work, send through the engineer's drawings with your enquiry.

Do you do earthquake strengthening on existing buildings?

Yes, it's a major part of what we do. We fabricate and install steel frames, moment frames and bracing to the engineer's specification. Your engineer sets the design and target %NBS; we build and install the steel to match.

What else can you make?

House beams and structural steel, balustrades and handrails, custom trailers, and one-off or short-run fabrication like brackets, frames, platforms and outdoor furniture. If it's steel and you're not sure, ask.

How long will my job take?

That depends on the size of the job, materials and the current workload. Tom will give you a timeframe along with your quote, so you know what to expect before you commit.
